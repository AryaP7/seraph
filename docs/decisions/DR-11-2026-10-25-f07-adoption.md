# DR-11 — F07 directed-network diagnostic: adoption of the proposed specification

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Status:** adopted by the owner
**Supersedes:** nothing. The proposal itself is unchanged.

## The decision

The owner adopted the existing F07 directed-network diagnostic specification
and authorised executing its single bounded fit, in their own words:

> "I approve adopting the existing F07 directed-network diagnostic
> specification and executing its single bounded fit."

with the standing constraint, same message:

> "Preserve the original scientific methodology, equations, model parameters,
> prediction algorithms, thresholds, and validation protocols. Do not change
> production behavior or composite risk calculations without my approval."

The hold recorded on 2026-10-22 ("owner explicitly chose keep network fit
pending. Do not fit it.") is released by this decision and only by it.

## What was adopted, and what was not

The proposal at
[`NETWORK-DIAGNOSTIC-DESIGN.md`](../evidence/engineering-closeout-2026-10-21/NETWORK-DIAGNOSTIC-DESIGN.md)
is adopted **unchanged**. That file is preserved exactly as written, per its
own instruction: "Preserve the proposal, record the choice, and create a
separate specification with `model_status=user_declared` before fitting once."

The adopted specification is therefore a separate artefact,
`prototype_runs/f07-specification-user-declared.json`, holding the proposal's
constants transcribed without alteration:

| setting | value | from the proposal |
|---|---|---|
| `decay` | 0.5 | "decay 0.5 per five-minute step (five-minute lag-state half-life)" |
| `dispersion` | 10 | "negative-binomial dispersion 10" |
| `l2` | 0.01 | "L2 coefficient 0.01" |
| `minimum_bars` | 750 | "at least 750 native bins" |
| `minimum_events` | 10 | "ten events per node" |
| `stationarity_bound` | 0.95 | "source-column sums bounded by 0.95" |
| `seasonality` | `training_tod` | "training-window time-of-day baseline" |
| `session_reset` | `true` | "session-open lag reset" |
| `display_threshold` | 0.001 | "display threshold 0.001, with the full matrix retained" |
| `model_status` | `user_declared` | the adoption gate in `fit_network` |
| `design_ref` | the proposal's path | so a fit names the design it came from |

Fit interval: **2019-10-01 through 2020-04-30**, as the proposal fixes it.
Node order: the sorted five sectors of the existing provisional tariff
basket. Matrix convention: `B[target, source]`.

Nothing else was adopted. In particular these remain exactly as the proposal
states them, and are **not** findings of this decision:

- the parameters are "illustrative diagnostic choices, not calibrated or
  established optimal financial parameters", and must not be tuned to produce
  a connected or visually attractive graph;
- the weights are "conditional excitation estimates, not causal transmission,
  loss probabilities, or validated early warnings";
- a zero-edge, unidentifiable or insufficient-support outcome is a **valid
  result** and is retained rather than retried with different settings.

## Isolation from production

The diagnostic does not enter production risk scoring. `fit_network` writes
only its own artefact through `seraph/pillars/hawkes/network.py`'s `save()`;
it touches no CSRS point, no fusion weight, no pillar vintage and no warning
threshold. The proposal's own limit applies: "only the explicit retrospective
network archive may display this fit", and the historical score and the
geographic news globe remain unchanged.

The owner's instruction above — do not change production behaviour or
composite risk calculations without approval — is therefore satisfied by
leaving the diagnostic where the design puts it, and no integration into the
composite was performed or implied.

## Reproducibility

The result records `input_sha256` over its own timestamps and counts, the
`specification` as adopted, `provenance` naming the real input path, and
`fitted_at` as an actual time. Re-running the same interval against the same
stored bars reproduces the same `input_sha256`; a different one means the
inputs changed, not the model.
